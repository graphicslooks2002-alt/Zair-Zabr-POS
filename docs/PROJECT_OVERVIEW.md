# Zair Zabar POS — Product Overview

**Run your restaurant from one screen.**
Take orders, manage tables, accept payments, and watch your sales grow — fast, reliable, and easy for your whole team.

## 📖 What is Zair Zabar POS?

Zair Zabar POS is a complete **Point-of-Sale platform built for restaurants, cafés, and food outlets.**
It replaces messy paper tickets and disconnected tools with a single, modern system your staff can learn in minutes — from the front counter to the kitchen to the manager's dashboard.

Everything updates **live**, so orders, tables, and sales are always in sync across every device.

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🧾 Smart Order Management
Create dine-in or takeaway orders in seconds, add items from the live menu, and track each order's status until it's settled. Nothing gets lost.

<div align="center">
<img src="images/create-order.png" alt="Create order" width="720"/>
</div>

### ✏️ Edit Orders Anytime
Customer changed their mind? Add a dish, bump a quantity, or remove an item from an order that's already placed — the bill and your sales totals update instantly and correctly. No voids, no re-typing the whole order.

<div align="center">
<img src="images/edit-order.png" alt="Edit order" width="720"/>
</div>

### 🪑 Real-Time Table Management
See which tables are free, booked, or occupied at a glance. Assign and free tables instantly as guests come and go.

<div align="center">
<img src="images/tables.png" alt="Tables" width="720"/>
</div>

### 📋 Easy Menu Control
Add, edit, or remove dishes and categories with photos and prices — no technical help needed. Changes appear instantly for the whole team.

<div align="center">
<img src="images/menu.png" alt="Menu management" width="720"/>
</div>

### 💳 Flexible Payments & Billing
Accept **online payments** or **cash**, auto-generate itemized bills, and keep a clear list of **pending payments** so no bill slips through.

<div align="center">
<img src="images/invoice.png" alt="Invoice" width="420"/>
</div>

### 📊 Business Analytics Dashboard
Know how the business is doing at any moment — **daily, weekly, and monthly sales**, payment breakdowns, and key performance numbers, all visual and easy to read.

<div align="center">
<img src="images/dashboard.png" alt="Dashboard" width="720"/>
</div>

### 👥 Staff Roles & Permissions
Give each team member exactly the access they need:
- **Admin** — full control: menu, staff, tables, and reports.
- **Cashier** — take orders and settle payments.
- **Waiter** — take orders and manage tables on the floor.

### 🔐 Secure Sign-In
Protected accounts with email verification, admin-approved staff onboarding, and safe password resets. Only the right people get in.

### ⏱️ Automatic Business Sessions
The system automatically groups each working day into a business session, so your sales reports are always accurate — even for late-night service.
